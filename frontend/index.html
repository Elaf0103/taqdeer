Platform Capabilities

Everything you need to grade at scale

From raw scanned answer sheets to structured reports — Taqdeer handles the complete evaluation pipeline so instructors can focus on teaching.

🤖

AI-Assisted Grading

Large language models evaluate open-ended answers against the rubric, producing consistent and explainable scores at every question level.

⚖️

Consensus-Based Evaluation

Multiple AI models are run in parallel and a consensus algorithm reconciles their scores — reducing model bias and improving reliability.

📊

Learning Gap Analysis

Automatically detect which topics or concepts students struggled with, ranked by severity, helping instructors target remedial instruction.

👩‍🏫

Teacher-in-the-Loop Review

Every AI-generated score is reviewable. Instructors can approve, override, or adjust before results are finalized — keeping full academic control.

📤

Report Exporting

Export graded results and performance analytics as structured reports in CSV format, ready for institutional record-keeping or further analysis.

🔍

OCR Answer Extraction

Scanned handwritten answer sheets are processed through an OCR pipeline, extracting student responses automatically before grading begins.

Workflow

From exam to insights in four steps

A streamlined, end-to-end process that takes you from raw answer sheets to reviewed, finalized grades.

1
📋

Upload Rubric & Answer Key

Define the exam rubric and upload the model answer key. Set scoring criteria for each question.

2
📁

Upload Student Answers

Upload scanned student answer sheets (PDF). The system anonymizes and extracts responses via OCR.

3
⚡

Run AI Grading

Select the AI models and trigger the grading run. Consensus scores are computed across all submissions.

4
✅

Review & Export Results

Inspect per-question scores, approve or adjust grades, run gap analysis, and export final reports.

Target Audience

Built for university instructors

Taqdeer is designed for academic staff who conduct and grade examinations — not a student-facing tool.

🎓

University Instructors

Course instructors and teaching assistants who need a reliable, efficient way to handle large-scale exam grading while maintaining full academic oversight.

  • Manage multiple exams and cohorts
  • Define custom rubrics per exam
  • Review and override AI scores
  • Track student performance over time
  • Export results for grade submission
🏛️

Academic Administrators

Department heads and academic coordinators who need aggregated performance data, learning analytics, and insights across courses and semesters.

  • View cross-course analytics
  • Identify systemic learning gaps
  • Manage instructor accounts
  • Access archived exam reports
  • Monitor grading workflows

Students do not directly interact with Taqdeer — results are communicated through the institution's normal channels.
